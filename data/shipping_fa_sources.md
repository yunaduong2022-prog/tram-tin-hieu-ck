# Shipping FA V1 — research only
- Source: user-supplied financials-industry_2000-2026.zip (annual) and financials-industry_2000-2026 (1).zip (quarterly). The installer contains only derived inputs, not original licensed exports. Use only under applicable data rights.
- TTM: Q4/2025 + Q1–Q3/2026; comparative Q4/2024 + Q1–Q3/2025. PVT FY_DIFF: annual FY2025 figures differ slightly from sum of quarterly records; the score consistently uses quarterly records, not audited substitution.
- Data in VND billion except shares, price proxy (VND), and ratios. EBITDA = operating EBIT (gross profit + selling/admin expenses in source sign convention) + D&A; ROE uses average parent equity, ROA average total assets and consolidated PAT, CFO/PAT consolidated. Net debt excludes only cash/equivalents, not term deposits. EV includes NCI.
- Snapshot price proxy = market cap/source shares; Stock Metrics period_date missing, so not dated EOD. Historic P/B = mean of up to 12 positive quarterly observations (at least 8), Q3/2023–Q2/2026, not daily rolling mean. Industry P/B arithmetic mean for eligible positive-equity peers within the same valuation mode. Rank by subindustry, >=3 complete peers. Prices below 9000 excluded; known latest EOD below floor also vetoes snapshot. EOD estimate only when shares match financial and source share counts within 1%, no CAP flag, date consistent with metadata and no duplicate or failed symbol. If unavailable, snapshot remains explicitly reference. No full FA if financial input absent.
- PDV CAP mismatch; VST negative equity. Other income and negative operating cash flow explicitly flagged. VNA and VST absent from prior EOD quality snapshot; grouping does not backfill prices. No normalization of one-offs in V1.
- No formal VIP ranking, no trading signal, no changes to port, bank, BDS or securities FA formulas.
